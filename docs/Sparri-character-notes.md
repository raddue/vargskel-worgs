Working notes for two Sparri brothers in the Lancer campaign. **Rurik** and **Sten** are provisional first names; player character names and details remain undecided. ## Canon anchors

- The Yggdrasil generation ship’s colonists first landed on Sparr around 1900u. Sparr is a brutal, storm-wracked ice world; early colonists struggled to survive in the Yuga Pocket before finding a route to the habitable equator and founding Ynn.
- Sparri time is often divided into “sagas,” cultural eras rather than fixed spans of years. The Dawn Saga and Familiekrig Saga are examples. The Familiekrig era of inter-clan violence is remembered as shameful and as a costly lesson; domstol grievance hearings and Ynn’s sanctuary status arose from that history.
- Sparri society is organized around clans and families. Chiefs have local authority; disputes may go to domstol or elders at Ynn, though younger Sparri may disregard these processes.
- Sparri storytelling celebrates exceptional people, personal strength (literal or figurative), resilience, and adventure. The culture has traditions that value deaths in battle or far from home, but this is not a requirement for every Sparri person.
- Clan, family, deeds, and major life events may be recorded through full-body tattoos. Designs vary by individual; technoshamans may include circuit diagrams or religious symbols, or wear technological components as jewelry.
- Ynnervan religious traditions changed after Union destroyed Ynneval. Some Sparri venerate machine-spirits; views on NHPs differ. Technoshamans combine religious practice with knowledge of technology and may become pilots.
- Sparri diaspora is widespread and culturally diverse. Mercenary work and close-quarters combat are recognizable associations, not universal traits.

## Additional video-transcript details (secondary source)

Transcript describes the Yuga Pocket’s early catastrophe and the years-long migration to Ynn, inter-clan conflict and the Familiekrig Saga, Prudent Interval’s presentation as Ynneval, Union’s attack on the temple-city, and later discovery of a vast subglacial ecosystem. The video frames spaceflight and diaspora as fitting a cultural narrative of exodus and exploration. Verify fine historical details against the Core Rulebook before treating them as firm canon.
