# THE OMNISCIENT OVERLORD

**AVERNA · Rurik "Daemon" Vargskel**

## Quick Guide

Choose one tab. The Chomolungma plan starts with Leader I, Technophile I and Iconoclast I. This is a hypothetical LL0 talent set; confirm changes against the fielded COMP/CON export. Servant and Unstable are installed at 0 SP each.

Reprint and rebuild at Full Repair when changing loadout, subject to GM approval of respecs.

### Why replace Hacker I?

The fielded export lists Hacker I and Spotter I. This hypothetical plan starts with Technophile I and Iconoclast I instead. Student replaces Servant at LL2; Enlightenment replaces Student at LL3 and does not count against the AI limit. OSIRIS occupies the regular AI slot. At LL6, Lesson of Shaping permits ATHENA alongside OSIRIS. Leader supplies Leadership Dice.

### Iconoclast and the laser powers

Dustgrave’s Iconoclast starts at LL0. Its Unstable NHP costs 0 SP and does not count against the AI limit. Memetic Spark costs a quick action once per round and deals 1 AP Energy within Range 3 plus 1 for each undestroyed NHP aboard. It deals 3 with Servant or Student and Unstable, 4 with Enlightenment, OSIRIS and Unstable, and 5 when ATHENA joins at LL6.

At LL1, Transmuting Spark follows an action granted by an NHP, including Memetic Spark. As a free action, it deals 2 AP Energy to chosen characters in a Line 3. Each NHP can trigger it once per round; handing over control does not trigger it. At LL4, repeated Transmuting Sparks charge Transcension. When it activates, Memetic Spark gains +4 damage and +5 Range through the end of Rurik’s next turn. The state also grants Hover 1 and immunity to involuntary movement.

### Lock On and reactions

Autopod is a Main Launcher, Unique, 1 SP, Range 15, Seeking, and 3 Kinetic. Its Autonomous Assault reaction triggers once per round when another character attacks a target within Range 15 of Rurik and consumes Lock On. The hit is automatic. Leader II’s command reaction competes with Autopod if used during the same turn. A Smart nexus attacks E-Defense using GRIT, not Tech Attack.

Chomolungma starts at Sensors 15, 10 HP, 7 base SP and two mounts. Goblin unlocks at Goblin II: Sensors 20, Tech Attack +2 and Liturgicode's +1 Accuracy on tech attacks, but 6 HP, 2 Repairs, Fragile and one Flex. Swallowtail unlocks at Swallowtail II: Sensors 20, Speed 6, 6 base SP, Flex plus Aux/Aux, and Prophetic Scanners (1/round, when **it** inflicts Lock On, target is Shredded through the end of its next turn). ATHENA's zone automatically applies Lock On; on the older Swallowtail chassis path, confirm with the GM whether those marks count as inflicted by Swallowtail. Its stationary Integrated Cloak's Invisibility ends when Swallowtail reacts.

### Equipment limits

SP is frame base + floor(SYSTEMS/2) + GRIT. GRIT is ceil(LL/2). Core bonuses arrive at LL3, LL6, LL9 and LL12. Improved Armament adds a Flex only to a frame with fewer than three non-integrated mounts. A Main/Aux takes a Main plus an Aux; a Flex takes a Main or two Aux; Aux/Aux takes two Aux. On the Chomolungma path, Hunter-Killer and Light Nexus share Main/Aux. Autopod takes the original Flex at LL1, while Improved Armament restores the Missile Rack pair on its bonus Flex at LL3. At LL7, Assault Rifle replaces Autopod; Blackspot replaces Assault Rifle at LL9. Integrated Weapon adds a separate Light Nexus at LL9. Never mount Hunter-Killer on Aux/Aux. The Chomolungma print installs Servant through LL1, Student at LL2, Enlightenment and OSIRIS from LL3, ATHENA from LL6, and Unstable from LL0. Enlightenment and Unstable do not use regular AI slots; The Lesson of Shaping permits OSIRIS and ATHENA together. You may choose a different available system after a Full Repair. The Chomolungma print shows one SP-checked installed set at each level; the Goblin tab has a separate progression.

### Chassis choices

Rurik stays in Chomolungma throughout this path. Goblin, Swallowtail, and Lich offer different chassis traits, but changing frames would replace Chomolungma’s Brilliance, Data Siphon, and Wide-Area Code Pulse. Lich I–II licenses at LL9–10 supply Unhinge Chronology without a frame change. Check any future alternate frame against the campaign’s COMP/CON catalog before printing it.

## Goblin Build

Averna changes to Goblin at LL2. The plan replaces the exported Hacker I with Technophile I as the LL1 reallocation; confirm the respec in COMP/CON. Held Image supports allied attacks, while ATHENA and the Cloaking Field arrive at LL6. Banish from Viral Logic Suite punishes voluntary movement; Puppet System's involuntary movement does not trigger it.

At LL11 the Unraveler and Law of Blades loadout keeps Custom Paint Job. For a Smartgun alternate, replace Unraveler with Smartgun and remove Law of Blades. Both alternatives use 17 SP at LL11. Unraveler deals Reliable 2 unless its damage would destroy or structure the target. The Cloaking Field costs 2 Heat to activate. Enlightenment does not count against the AI limit.

## Swallowtail Build

The scout tab prints original Swallowtail at LL3 after Goblin I and Swallowtail I–II. This gets Autopod at LL1 and the Shredded frame trait as early as LL3. Lock On from its own quick tech is reliable; neither a teammate's mark nor ATHENA's passive marking should be assumed to trigger Prophetic Scanners without a GM ruling. ATHENA costs 3 SP and occupies the AI slot. Full Subjectivity Sync adds 2 Evasion at LL6, while House Guard I lets the scout keep the adjacent-ally Spotter benefit within Range 2, if the GM applies its adjacency wording to Spotter I. The path retains the 15-space Autopod reach despite Sensors 20.
