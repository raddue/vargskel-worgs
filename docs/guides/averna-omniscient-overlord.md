# THE OMNISCIENT OVERLORD

**AVERNA · Rurik "Daemon" Vargskel · three independent LL0–LL12 support builds**

## Quick Guide

Choose one tab. Chomolungma keeps a durable command hull, Goblin maximizes tech control, and Swallowtail makes its own Lock On inflict Shredded. Each level above is a complete print, not a shared sequence. LL0 records the fielded Chomolungma: Hunter-Killer plus Light Nexus on Main/Aux, two Missile Racks on Flex; Personalizations, Turret Drones, Jump Jets, Shield and Paint fill 8 SP. The LL0 talent recommendation is a proposed respec to Spotter I, Leader I and Drone Commander I. Verify the current character sheet before applying it. Rurik has no licenses yet.

**Ranked choice:** 1. Chomolungma for resilient squad command; 2. Swallowtail for a fast mark-and-Shred scout; 3. Goblin for the strongest tech attacks with the smallest margin for error. The paths are independent. Reprint and rebuild at Full Repair when changing loadout, subject to GM approval of respecs.

### Why replace Hacker I?

Hacker I's SNOW_CRASH requires Rurik to hit with a tech attack that consumes Lock On. This build wants an ally to consume that mark, triggering Autopod. Drone Commander I instead gives Drone systems +5 HP and lets Shepherd move one drone up to 4 spaces as a protocol within Sensors. The existing Turret Drones benefit without consuming a mark. Spotter I lets an adjacent ally who consumes Lock On roll twice, and Leader I contributes Leadership Dice. House Guard I later stretches adjacency-dependent ally effects to Range 2. If Rurik starts consuming marks with Invade regularly, Hacker I becomes worth reconsidering; it costs the LL0 Drone Commander rank or delays the planned talent at a later LL.

### Lock On and reactions

Autopod is a Main Launcher, Unique, 1 SP, Range 15, Seeking, and 3 Kinetic. Its Autonomous Assault reaction triggers **once per round** when another character attacks a target within Range 15 of Rurik and consumes Lock On. The hit is automatic, with no attack roll. The attacker may use Spotter I if adjacent to Rurik. A Smart nexus still attacks using GRIT against E-Defense, not Tech Attack. Spotter III grants an adjacent ally an immediate quick action as their reaction when you Lock On; it can consume the new mark, but only an attack makes Autopod fire. That ally also needs their reaction available. Leader II's command reaction and Held Image compete with Autopod if used during the same turn; ordinary one-reaction-per-turn limits still apply. A stationary Spotter II can add a free Lock On at the end of Rurik's turn.

Chomolungma starts at Sensors 15, 10 HP, 7 base SP and two mounts. Goblin unlocks at Goblin II: Sensors 20, Tech Attack +2 and Liturgicode's +1 Accuracy on tech attacks, but 6 HP, 2 Repairs, Fragile and one Flex. Swallowtail unlocks at Swallowtail II: Sensors 20, Speed 6, 6 base SP, Flex plus Aux/Aux, and Prophetic Scanners (1/round, when **it** inflicts Lock On, target is Shredded through the end of its next turn). ATHENA's zone automatically applies Lock On; confirm with the GM whether those marks count as inflicted by Swallowtail. Held Image explicitly makes your Lock On action, but its reaction cannot also fire Autopod on that turn. The stationary Integrated Cloak's Invisibility ends when Swallowtail reacts.

### Equipment limits

SP is frame base + floor(SYSTEMS/2) + GRIT. GRIT is ceil(LL/2). Core bonuses arrive at LL3, LL6, LL9 and LL12. Improved Armament adds a Flex only to a frame with fewer than three non-integrated mounts. A Main/Aux takes a Main plus an Aux; a Flex takes a Main or two Aux; Aux/Aux takes two Aux. Thus Autopod plus Light Nexus fits Chomolungma's Main/Aux, Goblin's only original Flex takes Autopod alone, and Swallowtail's Flex takes Autopod while Aux/Aux holds both racks. Never mount Hunter-Killer on Aux/Aux. Technophile's Servant/Student/Enlightenment system is optional. Enlightenment does not count toward the installed AI limit, but these prints leave it off; ATHENA or OSIRIS is the only installed AI where listed. You may choose a different available system after a Full Repair, but each tab lists one legal installed set at each level.

### Other frames, ranked

1. **SSC Swallowtail (Ranger Variant)** (No Room for a Wallflower): alternative for terrain setup and ally movement on a mark. The main path uses original Swallowtail's Prophetic Scanners, so switching loses the mark-and-Shred payoff.
2. **HORUS Lich** (Long Rim): controller with stronger survival tricks, but requires a Lich II license detour before it can be printed. The listed paths buy Lich late for its systems.
3. **IPS-N Kidd** (No Room for a Wallflower): ally-support specialist; its frame requires Kidd II and trades away the chosen frame's tech traits.

These are frame alternatives, not extra installed systems or free unlocks. Frame variants must be checked against the campaign's exact COMP/CON catalog before swapping.

## Goblin Build

The tech-control tab prints Goblin at LL2. It trades Chomolungma's HP and mounts for better hacking. H0R_OS I adds Puppet System and Eject Power Cores; H0R_OS II adds constructs. Wandering Nightmare costs a Full Tech action and suppresses **all** reactions inside its zone, so keep it clear of allies relying on reaction attacks. Unhinge Chronology's Haste grants a free Boost 1/round until the beneficiary takes damage. OSIRIS is the late installed AI; ATHENA is unlocked at Swallowtail III but remains a swap, not a second AI. The build keeps Autopod on one Flex and adds a second Flex through Improved Armament.

## Swallowtail Build

The scout tab prints original Swallowtail at LL3 after Goblin I and Swallowtail I–II. This gets Autopod at LL1 and the Shredded frame trait as early as LL3. Lock On from its own quick tech is reliable; neither a teammate's mark nor ATHENA's passive marking should be assumed to trigger Prophetic Scanners without a GM ruling. ATHENA costs 3 SP and occupies the AI slot. Full Subjectivity Sync adds 2 Evasion at LL6, while House Guard I lets the scout keep the adjacent-ally Spotter benefit within Range 2, if the GM applies its adjacency wording to Spotter I. The path retains the 15-space Autopod reach despite Sensors 20.
