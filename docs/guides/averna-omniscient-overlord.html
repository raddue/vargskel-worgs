Abilities ▸
SPEED 4 · TECH +3 · WEAPON +0 · SENSORS 15

Quick actions

Boost

Move 4 spaces as a quick action. Your standard move also covers 4; both together cover 8.

Hide / Search

Hide when eligible; Search for a hidden target.

Skirmish

Lock On, Bolster, Scan, or Invade within Sensors 15. Lock On and Invade need line of sight; Invade: +3 vs E-Defense, 2 Heat on hit plus an available invasion option.

Quick Tech

Knock back or Prone a target, or hold one in a grapple. Mech attacks add +0 GRIT; saves use Save Target 11.

Ram / Grapple

Knock back or Prone a target; or hold one in a grapple.

Prepare / Shut Down / Activate

Prepare a quick action for a trigger; Shut Down to clear Heat and system effects; Activate installed quick-action gear.

Eject

Emergency quick action: launch 6 spaces; once per Full Repair.

Self-Destruct

Start a reactor meltdown. Explosion at end of your next turn or a chosen turn in the following two rounds; destroys the mech and pilot.

Chomolungma Invade

Balance Control Lockout: push 2 and Prone (Immobilize if already Prone, 1/scene per target); System Crusher: 4 total Heat and 4 Burn if it overheats (1/scene per target).

Turret Drones

Quick / reaction: Limited 3: deploy within Sensors and line of sight. 1/round per turret, an ally within Range 10 of it hits; turret deals 3 Kinetic to that target if it has line of sight.

Full actions

Barrage

Fire two weapons at +0, plus an eligible Aux on each fired mount. A pair of Aux weapons on the same mount can fire together; reload Loading weapons after use.

Full Tech

Take two Quick Tech options, including the same option twice, or one Full Tech system action.

Stabilize

Choose: clear all Heat/Exposed, or spend 1 Repair to restore all HP. Then reload all Loading weapons and choose one of: clear Burn, clear your eligible condition, or clear an adjacent ally’s eligible condition.

Disengage / Improvised Attack / Skill Check

Move without provoking reactions; strike without a weapon; or attempt a task with uncertain outcome.

Boot Up / Mount / Dismount

Restart after Shut Down; entering or leaving a mech takes a full action.

Wide-Area Code Pulse

Spend 1 Core Power to Invade any number of hostiles within Sensors, ignoring line of sight and Invisible; misses become Impaired.

Reactions

Overwatch

1/round: Skirmish at +0 when a hostile starts movement within a weapon’s Threat (normally 1).

Brace

Reduce an incoming hit; limits your next turn’s actions.

Protocols / free actions

Standard move

Move 4 spaces without spending a quick or full action.

Overcharge

Once per turn, take an extra quick action as a free action. Heat cost escalates 1, 1d3, 1d6, then 1d6+4 until Full Repair.

AI handoff

Protocol: hand control to an installed AI or take it back. While it controls the mech, you cannot take actions with that mech.

Type-3 Projected Shield

Protocol: Take 1 Heat (Self); choose a visible character. Ranged/melee attacks between you gain +2 Difficulty until your next turn.

Leader I

Free action: Issue an order and give an ally a Leadership Die for +1 Accuracy on the directed action.

On foot

Pilot movement and actions

HP 9, Armor 1, Evasion 8, E-Defense 8, Speed 4 (Assault Hardsuit). Share the mech’s action pool; Boost, Hide, Search, Activate, Skill Check, Disengage, Prepare, Mount and Overwatch remain available.

Fight / Jockey / Reload

Fight: full action with one pilot weapon. Jockey: full action against an adjacent mech. Reload one Loading pilot weapon as a quick action.

Pilot weapons

Medium Signature: Range 5, 2 damage. Light Signature: Range 3, 1 damage, Sidearm. Choose damage type on acquisition; pilot Fight uses GRIT.

Other gear

Assault Hardsuit, Dataplating, Sylph Undersuit, Personal Drone. No mech-scale attack bonuses.

Pilot triggers

Narrative skill checks use one applicable trigger, not mech attack rolls. Current trigger ranks appear above this panel.

Passives

Mech profile

HP 12, Evasion 8, E-Defense 12, Speed 4, Heat Cap 6, Repair Cap 4, Sensors 15, SP 8.

Attack and save bonuses

Weapon attacks +0; tech attacks +3; Save Target 11. Smart weapons attack E-Defense, but use weapon bonus, not tech attack.

Brilliance / Data Siphon

1/scene: free Quick Tech then free Bolster or Lock On. Tech attacks against hostiles also Scan them for free.

Replaceable Parts

While resting, repair structure at 1 Repair per damage instead of 2.

Personalizations

Passive: +2 HP and a GM-approved minor modification; possible +1 Accuracy to a relevant skill check.

Rapid Burst Jump Jet System

Boost: Fly during a Boost; end on a solid surface or fall.

Custom Paint Job

Passive: On structure damage, roll 1d6; on 6, return to 1 HP and ignore that damage. Once per Full Repair.

Servant-Class NHP

Passive: 0 SP; installed AI can take control when you hand over the mech as a protocol.

Spotter I

Passive: Adjacent ally consuming your target’s Lock On rolls twice and chooses.

Technophile I

AI: Servant-Class NHP may pilot independently when given control.

Why replace Hacker I?

Hacker I's SNOW_CRASH requires a tech attack that consumes Lock On. Rurik chose Technophile I instead and installs its 0-SP Servant-Class NHP at LL0. OSIRIS replaces Servant at LL3. At LL6, Technophile III grants the 0-SP Enlightenment-Class NHP, which does not count against the AI limit; The Lesson of Shaping allows OSIRIS and ATHENA to occupy two regular AI slots. Spotter I helps an adjacent ally spending Lock On, and Leader I contributes Leadership Dice. House Guard I extends adjacency-based effects to Range 2 at LL10.

Iconoclast and the laser powers

Dustgrave’s Iconoclast starts at LL7. Its Unstable NHP costs 0 SP and does not count against the AI limit, so Rurik can install it with OSIRIS, ATHENA, and Enlightenment. Unlike Enlightenment, Unstable can cascade. Memetic Spark costs a quick action once per round: it deals 1 AP Energy damage within Range 3, plus 1 for each undestroyed NHP aboard, including Unstable. With all four aboard, it deals 5 AP Energy.

At LL8, Transmuting Spark follows an action granted by an NHP, such as OSIRIS’s Hurl Into the Duat or ATHENA’s Simulacrum. As a free action, it deals 2 AP Energy to chosen characters in a Line 3. Each NHP can trigger it once per round; handing control to an NHP does not trigger it. At LL9, repeated Transmuting Sparks charge Transcension. When it activates, Memetic Spark gains +4 damage and +5 Range through the end of Rurik’s next turn: 9 AP Energy at Range 8 with four NHPs aboard. The state also grants Hover 1 and immunity to involuntary movement. The close range and quick-action cost make Memetic Spark a situational shot on Chomolungma, not a replacement for its Sensors 15 tech actions.

Lock On and reactions

Autopod is a Main Launcher, Unique, 1 SP, Range 15, Seeking, and 3 Kinetic. Its Autonomous Assault reaction triggers once per round when another character attacks a target within Range 15 of Rurik and consumes Lock On. The hit is automatic, with no attack roll. The attacker may use Spotter I if adjacent to Rurik. A Smart nexus still attacks using GRIT against E-Defense, not Tech Attack. Spotter III grants an adjacent ally an immediate quick action as their reaction when you Lock On; it can consume the new mark, but only an attack makes Autopod fire. That ally also needs their reaction available. Leader II's command reaction competes with Autopod if used during the same turn; ordinary one-reaction-per-turn limits still apply. A stationary Spotter II can add a free Lock On at the end of Rurik's turn. This Autopod interaction applies only through LL6.

Equipment limits

SP is frame base + floor(SYSTEMS/2) + GRIT. GRIT is ceil(LL/2). Core bonuses arrive at LL3, LL6, LL9 and LL12. Improved Armament adds a Flex only to a frame with fewer than three non-integrated mounts. A Main/Aux takes a Main plus an Aux; a Flex takes a Main or two Aux; Aux/Aux takes two Aux. On the Chomolungma path, Hunter-Killer and Light Nexus share Main/Aux. Autopod takes the original Flex at LL1, while Improved Armament restores the Missile Rack pair on its bonus Flex at LL3. At LL7, Assault Rifle replaces Autopod; Blackspot replaces Assault Rifle at LL9. Integrated Weapon adds a separate Light Nexus at LL9. Never mount Hunter-Killer on Aux/Aux. The Chomolungma print installs Servant until LL2, OSIRIS from LL3, OSIRIS with ATHENA and Enlightenment from LL6, and Unstable from LL7. SlowedCannot Boost, or make any special move granted by talents, systems, or weapons.
JammedCannot use comms, make attacks other than Improvised Attack, Grapple or Ram, or take reactions.

HiddenCannot be targeted by hostile attacks or actions, and does not cause Engagement.
EngagedRanged attacks made by this character gain +1 Difficulty, and all additional movement is lost.
ExposedAll Kinetic, Explosive, or Energy damage taken by this character is doubled.
Shut DownWhile Shut Down, mechs are Stunned indefinitely.
SmartAttacks with this weapon use the target's E-Defense instead of Evasion (no E-Defense counts as 8).
SeekingIgnores cover and does not require line of sight, so long as a path to the target can be drawn.
