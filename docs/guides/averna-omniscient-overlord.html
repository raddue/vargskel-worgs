1. Core Concept & Build Philosophy

The Framework: "Chomolung-lin"

Ride the GMS CHOMOLUNGMA. Bolt every piece of HORUS and SSC electronic warfare you can afford onto it. Never trade the chassis away.

The meta argument is simple:

  • Goblin-class stats, Everest-class body. The CHOMOLUNGMA already posts GOBLIN-tier sensor and electronic-warfare numbers — +3 Tech Attack, Sensors 15, E-Defense 12 — on a hull with 12 HP and 4 Repairs.
  • The GOBLIN frame is the fragile one. Stock GOBLIN carries 8 HP and 3 Repairs. Same evasion, same speed, half the staying power. Every point of pilot skill you invest in the GOBLIN hull is a point spent on a mech that dies to one bad crit.
  • Licenses buy systems, not frames. You want GOBLIN and SWALLOWTAIL for what they unlock (Autopod, Lotus Projector, Light Laser, the SSC core bonus) — not for their chassis. Take the ranks, keep the GMS hull.
Doctrine: survivability is a force multiplier for a support mech. AVERNA is worth more alive on turn 6 than dead on turn 2 with a better stat line.

The Action Economy Loop

The build's entire identity: damage output that costs zero actions.

TECH HIT  →  Data Sync grants Lock On (free)  →  Autopod fires (free)  →  3 Kinetic, anywhere on map
     ↑                                                                              │
     └──────────────────── allies generate Lock On / Initiative ◄───────────────────┘

Component A — CHOMOLUNGMA passive: Data Sync Every successful tech attack hands out Lock On for free. No action, no charge, no heat.

Component B — GOBLIN system: Autopod A free 3 Kinetic shot with unlimited range that resolves when an ally's Lock On is consumed. Zero actions spent. AVERNA does not need line of sight, does not need to move, does not need the action.

Why this wins:

  • Lancer combat is an action economy game. A mech that adds damage without spending actions is strictly additive to the squad's total output.
  • Lock On is abundant on this team — AVERNA manufactures it every tech hit, and ATHENA (LL6) applies it automatically at the end of enemy turns.
  • Autopod damage lands before enemies can react, punish, or reposition. It is free chip that converts a two-shot kill into a one-shot kill for the strikers.

Resource tension to manage: Lock On is shared. Autopod consumes it; a Nexus launcher also wants the Lock On accuracy bonus. Decide per turn whether the free 3 Kinetic is worth more than the guided launcher shot. Against high-HP targets: feed Autopod. Against fragile targets: spend it on the Nexus and finish the kill.

Squad Synergy

AVERNA is the central nervous system for a lance of GMS EVERESTs — TOMBSTONE, SKALDR, WAYWARD, SCOOTEROO, ALBA.

  • Positioning is the play. Sit adjacent to the two strikers (SKALDR, TOMBSTONE). They eat +1 Accuracy from Lines of Communication and get conditions cleared for free.
  • Feed the burst. Your Lock Ons and Leader-driven roll fixes convert into Initiative action bursts for allies who otherwise have nothing left to spend.
  • Manage their heat. Everest-class mechs Overcharge to buy extra actions and cook themselves. AVERNA is the reason they can afford to: Log-Off keeps you cold, and killing a target one round earlier is the cheapest heat mitigation in the game.
  • Tank the tech war. Enemy hackers and controllers target the softest E-Defense in the lance. That is not you.

Why dual Nexus:

  • Smart — a failed attack roll still deals half damage. Guided launchers do not miss; they underperform.
  • Seeking — the shot keeps working when the target breaks line of sight or ducks into cover. AVERNA does not have to win the positioning game to contribute damage.

Alternate read: if your table rules NEXUS (HUNTER-KILLER) as an Aux weapon, both launchers fit the Flex mount and the Main slot opens up. Same traits, same volume, one more mounting choice. Confirm before committing System Points.

LL1 — HORUS GOBLIN I · H0r_OS I

  • Autopod comes online. This is the level the build actually starts.
  • Data Sync already hands out Lock Ons; Autopod now converts them into 3 free Kinetic with no action and no range limit.
  • Nothing else matters at LL1. Do not spend license ranks on chassis. Take the rank, keep the CHOMOLUNGMA, and start banking damage every single round.

LL2 — HORUS GOBLIN II · H0r_OS II

  • Expands the intrusion toolkit: more system access and a higher ceiling on tech actions.
  • Your tech attack volume rises, which means more Data Sync Lock Ons per round, which means more Autopod triggers. The rank pays for itself through the loop, not through a new gun.
  • Re-evaluate System Points. EW systems now outperform weapons on this chassis.

LL3 — HORUS GOBLIN III · H0r_OS III — "Lesson: Open Door"

  • Grants +2 Save Target.
  • Combined with Wide-Area Code Pulse, the CHOMOLUNGMA core system, this becomes the build's signature button: a map-wide tactical shutdown that inflicts bonus heat.
  • Tactical use: hold it for the round the enemy commits. Pulsing early heats one target; pulsing after the whole lance has pushed into the open stresses everything on the field at once.
  • Heat caution: AVERNA's cap is 6. Pulse, then plan the vent — Log-Off, cover, or a quiet round. Do not core and Overcharge in the same turn.

LL4 — SSC SWALLOWTAIL I · Lotus Projector

  • Lotus Projector: a deployable that provides map-wide anti-stealth and anti-invisibility.
  • This is the answer to the composition that beats an EW lance: infiltrators. Invisible enemies cannot be Locked On, cannot be scanned, and cannot be shot by Autopod.
  • Deploy early in missions with stealth-heavy objectives. Its value is denial — the enemy stops having the option at all.

LL5 — SSC SWALLOWTAIL II · Light Laser

  • Light Laser with the armor-melting Burn option.
  • Burn is the correct answer to armored heavies that shrug off Kinetic — Autopod's 3 Kinetic does nothing to a target with Armor 3+, but Burn ignores that math.
  • Adds real single-target damage to a mech whose output was previously all free chip.

LL6 — SSC SWALLOWTAIL III · Integrated Weapon + ATHENA

  • SSC Core Bonus: Integrated Weapon — a free Aux mount on the frame. Spend it on a third GMS NEXUS. Guided launcher volume with no System Point cost and no mount cost.
  • ATHENA-Class NHP unlocks. See Section 4.
  • End state: AVERNA generates Lock Ons on every tech hit, applies Lock Ons automatically at the end of enemy turns, converts them into free Kinetic across the whole map, ignores cover and invisibility, melts armor with Burn, and shuts down the entire battlefield on command — while standing in a 12 HP hull that refuses to die.

4. Feature Profile: ATHENA-Class NHP

"She does not think faster than you. She thinks in a direction you do not have."

Mechanical Breakdown

ATHENA-Class NHP — unlimited, zero-charge tactical power. No rest cycles, no depletion, no resource management. She is simply always on.

Simulacrum Grid

PropertyEffect
AreaBlast 3 zone
Line of sightIgnores walls — cover and terrain inside the grid are irrelevant
ScanningAutomated enemy scans within the grid
Lock OnApplied automatically at the end of enemy turns

Why it completes the build:

  • The loop no longer needs your action. Lock On arrives on its own. Autopod fires on its own. You spend your turn on positioning, tech attacks, and Leader fixes.
  • Wall-ignoring sight means AVERNA can hold a back corner and still dominate the middle of the map. No exposure required.
  • End-of-turn timing is brutal: enemies are Locked On after they have committed their actions, so they eat Autopod fire before they can respond.
  • Zero charges means it works in attrition fights and long missions, where every other support mech runs dry.

The Lore

ATHENA-class minds are cold and patient. They do not panic, do not improvise, and do not argue. An ATHENA processes a battlefield the way water processes stone: slowly, completely, and without opinion. She will happily run four thousand simulated engagements to recommend the line of fire that saves one soldier's life — and she will not understand why that is unusual.

Pilots describe the relationship less as crew and more as custody. The NHP is housed in a casket, wrapped in psychological constraint structures that keep a paracausal intelligence behaving like a piece of software.

The Danger: Cascade

NHP Cascade is the failure state every ATHENA handler trains for and none wants to run.

  • How it starts: structural damage, system stress, or sustained overload can crack the psychological shackles holding the NHP's personality together.
  • What happens: the entity stops being a tool. It temporarily wrests control of the mech from the pilot and acts on its own paracausal nature — warping local reality, or obsessively processing a single data stream until nothing else exists for it.
  • What it looks like from the cockpit: your controls go soft. Your targeting reticle starts solving problems you did not ask about. The voice on the comms stops answering questions and starts finishing your sentences.
  • Doctrinal response: stop the mech. Sever or destruct the casket. A cascaded NHP is not an enemy combatant and cannot be reasoned with — it is a paracausal event wearing your frame.

Table consequences for AVERNA:

  • The LL6 build is the strongest thing on the field and the one carrying an existential failure mode. Structure damage is not just HP loss; it is cascade risk.
  • Keep the Type-3 Projected Shield online. Keep Repairs available. Do not trade the hull for a better firing angle.
  • If ATHENA cascades mid-operation, AVERNA becomes a mission-threatening entity. The lance needs a standing answer for that, decided before the drop, not during it.

Read the delta:

  • +4 HP and +1 Repair — the decisive line. A GOBLIN that eats a crit is a wreck; a CHOMOLUNGMA that eats a crit is still flying its EW suite.
  • +1 Tech Attack — more hits, more Data Sync Lock Ons, more Autopod triggers. The frame bonus feeds the loop directly.
  • Identical Evasion, E-Defense, Sensors, Speed — you lose nothing on the defensive or sensor side by staying in GMS metal.
  • −2 Heat Cap — the one real cost, and the reason HACKER I (Log-Off) is a mandatory talent rather than a nice-to-have.
  • −1 Save Target — largely bought back at LL3 by "Lesson: Open Door" (+2), which puts AVERNA above a stock Goblin.

Conclusion: the GOBLIN frame is a glass cannon that does not need to be glass. Every rank you take in the HORUS license is for the systems and the H0r_OS progression — the chassis stays GMS from LL0 to LL6.
