Quick Guide

Choose one tab. Chomolungma keeps a durable command hull, Goblin maximizes tech control, and Swallowtail makes its own Lock On inflict Shredded. Each level above is a complete print, not a shared sequence. LL0 records the fielded Chomolungma: Hunter-Killer plus Light Nexus on Main/Aux, two Missile Racks on Flex; Personalizations, Turret Drones, Jump Jets, Shield and Paint fill 8 SP. The LL0 talent recommendation is a proposed respec to Spotter I, Leader I and Drone Commander I. Verify the current character sheet before applying it. Rurik has no licenses yet.

Ranked choice: 1. Chomolungma for resilient squad command; 2. Swallowtail for a fast mark-and-Shred scout; 3. Goblin for the strongest tech attacks with the smallest margin for error. The paths are independent. Reprint and rebuild at Full Repair when changing loadout, subject to GM approval of respecs.

Why replace Hacker I?

Hacker I's SNOW_CRASH requires Rurik to hit with a tech attack that consumes Lock On. This build wants an ally to consume that mark, triggering Autopod. Drone Commander I instead gives Drone systems +5 HP and lets Shepherd move one drone up to 4 spaces as a protocol within Sensors. The existing Turret Drones benefit without consuming a mark. Spotter I lets an adjacent ally who consumes Lock On roll twice, and Leader I contributes Leadership Dice. House Guard I later stretches adjacency-dependent ally effects to Range 2. If Rurik starts consuming marks with Invade regularly, Hacker I becomes worth reconsidering; it costs the LL0 Drone Commander rank or delays the planned talent at a later LL.

Lock On and reactions

Autopod is a Main Launcher, Unique, 1 SP, Range 15, Seeking, and 3 Kinetic. Its Autonomous Assault reaction triggers once per round when another character attacks a target within Range 15 of Rurik and consumes Lock On. The hit is automatic, with no attack roll. The attacker may use Spotter I if adjacent to Rurik. A Smart nexus still attacks using GRIT against E-Defense, not Tech Attack. Spotter III grants an adjacent ally an immediate quick action as their reaction when you Lock On; it can consume the new mark, but only an attack makes Autopod fire. That ally also needs their reaction available. Leader II's command reaction and Held Image compete with Autopod if used during the same turn; ordinary one-reaction-per-turn limits still apply. A stationary Spotter II can add a free Lock On at the end of Rurik's turn.

Chomolungma starts at Sensors 15, 10 HP, 7 base SP and two mounts. Goblin unlocks at Goblin II: Sensors 20, Tech Attack +2 and Liturgicode's +1 Accuracy on tech attacks, but 6 HP, 2 Repairs, Fragile and one Flex. Swallowtail unlocks at Swallowtail II: Sensors 20, Speed 6, 6 base SP, Flex plus Aux/Aux, and Prophetic Scanners (1/round, when it inflicts Lock On, target is Shredded through the end of its next turn). ATHENA's zone automatically applies Lock On; confirm with the GM whether those marks count as inflicted by Swallowtail. Held Image explicitly makes your Lock On action, but its reaction cannot also fire Autopod on that turn. The stationary Integrated Cloak's Invisibility ends when Swallowtail reacts.

Equipment limits

SP is frame base + floor(SYSTEMS/2) + GRIT. GRIT is ceil(LL/2). Core bonuses arrive at LL3, LL6, LL9 and LL12. Improved Armament adds a Flex only to a frame with fewer than three non-integrated mounts. A Main/Aux takes a Main plus an Aux; a Flex takes a Main or two Aux; Aux/Aux takes two Aux. Thus Autopod plus Light Nexus fits Chomolungma's Main/Aux, Goblin's only original Flex takes Autopod alone, and Swallowtail's Flex takes Autopod while Aux/Aux holds both racks. Never mount Hunter-Killer on Aux/Aux. Technophile's Servant/Student/Enlightenment system is optional. Enlightenment does not count toward the installed AI limit, but these prints leave it off; ATHENA or OSIRIS is the only installed AI where listed. You may choose a different available system after a Full Repair, but each tab lists one legal installed set at each level.

Other frames, ranked

1. SSC Swallowtail (Ranger Variant) (No Room for a Wallflower): alternative for terrain setup and ally movement on a mark. The main path uses original Swallowtail's Prophetic Scanners, so switching loses the mark-and-Shred payoff. 2. HORUS Lich (Long Rim): controller with stronger survival tricks, but requires a Lich II license detour before it can be printed. The listed paths buy Lich late for its systems. 3. IPS-N Kidd (No Room for a Wallflower): ally-support specialist; its frame requires Kidd II and trades away the chosen frame's tech traits.

These are frame alternatives, not extra installed systems or free unlocks. Frame variants must be checked against the campaign's exact COMP/CON catalog before swapping.

Glossary

Involuntary MovementForced movement in a straight line. It does not provoke reactions or Engagement unless specified otherwise, but is still blocked by obstructions.
Save TargetWhen you force a save, the target must match or beat your mech's Save Target or take the consequences. GRIT is added to it.
Tech AttackAdded to tech attacks instead of GRIT. A tech attack is rolled against the target's E-Defense.
ImpairedReceives +1 Difficulty on all attacks, saves, and skill checks.
SlowedCannot Boost, or make any special move granted by talents, systems, or weapons.
JammedCannot use comms, make attacks other than Improvised Attack, Grapple or Ram, or take reactions.
ImmobilizedCannot make any voluntary movement.
ProneAttacks against it gain +1 Accuracy. It is Slowed and counts as moving in difficult terrain.
Lock OnA hostile character can consume it for +1 Accuracy on their next attack against this character.
ShreddedDoes not benefit from Armor or Resistance.
InvisibleAttacks against it have a 50 percent chance to miss before the attack roll is made.
HiddenCannot be targeted by hostile attacks or actions, and does not cause Engagement.
EngagedRanged attacks made by this character gain +1 Difficulty, and all additional movement is lost.
ExposedAll Kinetic, Explosive, or Energy damage taken by this character is doubled.
Shut DownWhile Shut Down, mechs are Stunned indefinitely.
SmartAttacks with this weapon use the target's E-Defense instead of Evasion (no E-Defense counts as 8).
SeekingIgnores cover and does not require line of sight, so long as a path to the target can be drawn.
LoadingMust be reloaded after each use. Mechs reload with the Stabilize action.
BlastAffects characters within its radius of a point within Range and line of sight.
UniqueCannot be duplicated: a character can only have one copy installed at a time.
AIOne AI system per mech. AIs cannot benefit from talents, and have a small chance of cascading when they take structure or stress damage.
GRITHalf your LL, rounded up. Added to your mech's HP, SP, attack rolls and Save Target.
E-DefenseHow hard this mech is to hit with electronic and guided weapons and systems.
EvasionHow hard this mech is to hit with ranged and melee attacks.
Wide-Area Code PulseThe Chomolungma core's 1/mission Full action: an Invade tech attack against any number of hostiles within Sensors, ignoring Invisible and line of sight. A miss leaves them Impaired until the end of their next turn.
Balance Control LockoutChomolungma core Invade option: push the target 2 spaces and knock them Prone. If they are already Prone, they are Immobilized until the end of their next turn. 1/scene per character.
System CrusherChomolungma core Invade option: your target takes an additional 2 Heat, for 4 total, and if that exceeds their Heat Cap they take 4 Burn as well. 1/scene per character.
Data SiphonChomolungma trait: a tech attack against a hostile character also Scans them, for free.
BrillianceChomolungma trait, 1/scene: take any Quick Tech action as a free action, then either Bolster or Lock On as a free action.
Replaceable PartsChomolungma trait: while resting, repair at 1 Repair per point of structure damage instead of 2.
SensorsYour mech's sensor range: the reach of tech actions, Lock On and most systems.
AutopodGOBLIN I weapon (1 SP, Main Launcher, Seeking, Unique, Range 15, 3 Kinetic). It makes no attack roll; it grants the Autonomous Assault reaction.
Autonomous AssaultThe Autopod's reaction, 1/round: another character attacks a target within Range 15 of you and consumes Lock On, and you automatically hit that target.
Puppet SystemGOBLIN I Invade option: the target moves its maximum Speed in a direction of your choice. Involuntary, but it provokes reactions and Engagement.
Eject Power CoresGOBLIN I Invade option: the target is Jammed until the end of their next turn, and characters adjacent to them take 2 Energy. 1/scene per character.
H0R_OS System Upgrade IGOBLIN I system (2 SP, Unique) that adds the Invade options Puppet System and Eject Power Cores.
H0R_OS System Upgrade IIGOBLIN II system (2 SP, Unique) that adds the Quick Tech options Construct Other: Ideal Image and False Idol.
H0R_OS System Upgrade IIIGOBLIN III system (2 SP, Unique) that adds the Invade options Dimensional Emblems and Celestial Shackles.
Construct OtherH0R_OS II Quick Tech: manifest an Other. Ideal Image is a Size 2 data construct that provides hard cover and blocks line of sight; False Idol is a decoy that forces a Systems save on anyone attacking the real target.
Dimensional EmblemsH0R_OS III Invade option: three Size 1 data constructs around the target. Passing through one deals 2 Heat and destroys it.
Celestial ShacklesH0R_OS III Invade option: mark the target's space. If they leave it, you may teleport them back once during your turn as a free action; a Systems save as a quick action breaks the effect.
MetahookGOBLIN II system (2 SP, Unique). The Goblin's recursive processing weave, sharpening weaponised code into an attack or softening it into shielding for allies.
OSIRIS-Class NHPGOBLIN III system (3 SP, AI, Unique) that adds the Hurl Into the Duat Quick Tech option.
ATHENA-Class NHPSWALLOWTAIL III system (3 SP, AI, Unique) that grants the AI tag and Simulacrum.
SimulacrumATHENA's Quick action: a Blast 3 area within Range 50 where you have full visibility, hostiles lose cover and cannot Hide or turn Invisible, and anyone ending their turn inside gains Lock On. Lasts the scene.
Lotus ProjectorSWALLOWTAIL I system (2 SP): a scout drone within Sensors and line of sight that emits a Burst 2 field telling you the location, HP, Evasion, E-Defense and Heat of everyone inside, and where hostiles cannot Hide or turn Invisible.
MarkerlightSWALLOWTAIL I system (2 SP): the marking tool that makes every subsequent shot against the target cheaper.
Retractable ProfileSWALLOWTAIL II system (1 SP, Unique): strips extraneous profile, tunes broadcast software and masks heat signatures.

Goblin Build

The tech-control tab prints Goblin at LL2. It trades Chomolungma's HP and mounts for better hacking. H0R_OS I adds Puppet System and Eject Power Cores; H0R_OS II adds constructs. Wandering Nightmare costs a Full Tech action and suppresses all reactions inside its zone, so keep it clear of allies relying on reaction attacks. Unhinge Chronology's Haste grants a free Boost 1/round until the beneficiary takes damage. OSIRIS is the late installed AI; ATHENA is unlocked at Swallowtail III but remains a swap, not a second AI. The build keeps Autopod on one Flex and adds a second Flex through Improved Armament.

Swallowtail Build

The scout tab prints original Swallowtail at LL3 after Goblin I and Swallowtail I–II. This gets Autopod at LL1 and the Shredded frame trait as early as LL3. Lock On from its own quick tech is reliable; neither a teammate's mark nor ATHENA's passive marking should be assumed to trigger Prophetic Scanners without a GM ruling. ATHENA costs 3 SP and occupies the AI slot. Full Subjectivity Sync adds 2 Evasion at LL6, while House Guard I lets the scout keep the adjacent-ally Spotter benefit within Range 2, if the GM applies its adjacency wording to Spotter I. The path retains the 15-space Autopod reach despite Sensors 20.
