Why replace Hacker I?

Hacker I's SNOW_CRASH requires a tech attack that consumes Lock On. Rurik chose Technophile I instead and installs its 0-SP Servant-Class NHP at LL0. OSIRIS replaces Servant at LL3. At LL6, Technophile III grants the 0-SP Enlightenment-Class NHP, which does not count against the AI limit; The Lesson of Shaping allows OSIRIS and ATHENA to occupy two regular AI slots. Spotter I helps an adjacent ally spending Lock On, and Leader I contributes Leadership Dice. House Guard I extends adjacency-based effects to Range 2 at LL10.

Iconoclast and the laser powers

Dustgrave’s Iconoclast starts at LL7. Its Unstable NHP costs 0 SP and does not count against the AI limit, so Rurik can install it with OSIRIS, ATHENA, and Enlightenment. Unlike Enlightenment, Unstable can cascade. Memetic Spark costs a quick action once per round: it deals 1 AP Energy damage within Range 3, plus 1 for each undestroyed NHP aboard, including Unstable. With all four aboard, it deals 5 AP Energy.

At LL8, Transmuting Spark follows an action granted by an NHP, such as OSIRIS’s Hurl Into the Duat or ATHENA’s Simulacrum. As a free action, it deals 2 AP Energy to chosen characters in a Line 3. Each NHP can trigger it once per round; handing control to an NHP does not trigger it. At LL9, repeated Transmuting Sparks charge Transcension. When it activates, Memetic Spark gains +4 damage and +5 Range through the end of Rurik’s next turn: 9 AP Energy at Range 8 with four NHPs aboard. The state also grants Hover 1 and immunity to involuntary movement. The close range and quick-action cost make Memetic Spark a situational shot on Chomolungma, not a replacement for its Sensors 15 tech actions.

Lock On and reactions

Autopod is a Main Launcher, Unique, 1 SP, Range 15, Seeking, and 3 Kinetic. Its Autonomous Assault reaction triggers once per round when another character attacks a target within Range 15 of Rurik and consumes Lock On. The hit is automatic, with no attack roll. The attacker may use Spotter I if adjacent to Rurik. A Smart nexus still attacks using GRIT against E-Defense, not Tech Attack. Spotter III grants an adjacent ally an immediate quick action as their reaction when you Lock On; it can consume the new mark, but only an attack makes Autopod fire. That ally also needs their reaction available. Leader II's command reaction competes with Autopod if used during the same turn; ordinary one-reaction-per-turn limits still apply. A stationary Spotter II can add a free Lock On at the end of Rurik's turn. This Autopod interaction applies only through LL6.

Chomolungma starts at Sensors 15, 10 HP, 7 base SP and two mounts. Goblin unlocks at Goblin II: Sensors 20, Tech Attack +2 and Liturgicode's +1 Accuracy on tech attacks, but 6 HP, 2 Repairs, Fragile and one Flex. Swallowtail unlocks at Swallowtail II: Sensors 20, Speed 6, 6 base SP, Flex plus Aux/Aux, and Prophetic Scanners (1/round, when it inflicts Lock On, target is Shredded through the end of its next turn). ATHENA's zone automatically applies Lock On; on the older Swallowtail chassis path, confirm with the GM whether those marks count as inflicted by Swallowtail. Its stationary Integrated Cloak's Invisibility ends when Swallowtail reacts.

Equipment limits

SP is frame base + floor(SYSTEMS/2) + GRIT. GRIT is ceil(LL/2). Core bonuses arrive at LL3, LL6, LL9 and LL12. Improved Armament adds a Flex only to a frame with fewer than three non-integrated mounts. A Main/Aux takes a Main plus an Aux; a Flex takes a Main or two Aux; Aux/Aux takes two Aux. On the Chomolungma path, Hunter-Killer and Light Nexus share Main/Aux. Autopod takes the original Flex at LL1, while Improved Armament restores the Missile Rack pair on its bonus Flex at LL3. At LL7, Assault Rifle replaces Autopod; Blackspot replaces Assault Rifle at LL9. Integrated Weapon adds a separate Light Nexus at LL9. Never mount Hunter-Killer on Aux/Aux. The Chomolungma print installs Servant until LL2, OSIRIS from LL3, OSIRIS with ATHENA and Enlightenment from LL6, and Unstable from LL7. Enlightenment and Unstable do not use regular AI slots; The Lesson of Shaping permits OSIRIS and ATHENA together. You may choose a different available system after a Full Repair. The Chomolungma print shows one SP-checked installed set at each level; the other two tabs await rebuild.

Wandering Nightmare costs a Full Tech action and suppresses all reactions inside its zone, so keep it clear of allies relying on reaction attacks. Unhinge Chronology's Haste grants a free Boost 1/round until the beneficiary takes damage. OSIRIS is the late installed AI; ATHENA is unlocked at Swallowtail III but remains a swap, not a second AI. The build keeps Autopod on one Flex and adds a second Flex through Improved Armament.

Swallowtail Build

The scout tab prints original Swallowtail at LL3 after Goblin I and Swallowtail I–II. This gets Autopod at LL1 and the Shredded frame trait as early as LL3. Lock On from its own quick tech is reliable; neither a teammate's mark nor ATHENA's passive marking should be assumed to trigger Prophetic Scanners without a GM ruling. ATHENA costs 3 SP and occupies the AI slot. Full Subjectivity Sync adds 2 Evasion at LL6, while House Guard I lets the scout keep the adjacent-ally Spotter benefit within Range 2, if the GM applies its adjacency wording to Spotter I. The path retains the 15-space Autopod reach despite Sensors 20.
