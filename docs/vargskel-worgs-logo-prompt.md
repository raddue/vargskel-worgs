# Vargskel Worgs logo prompt

## Main prompt

Create a restrained military unit patch for a small Lancer security and escort company. Use a strict, unmistakable shield silhouette: flat horizontal top edge, straight sides, and a single centered point at the bottom, like a traditional heraldic heater shield. Keep the outer contour simple and uninterrupted; no tabs, wings, shoulders, notches, or attached banners. Add one clean ice-blue border just inside the shield edge. Within the shield, show the worg from the reference image: a full-body, low, forward-charging worg with bared teeth and extended claws, angled slightly toward the viewer. Preserve its distinctive pose and fierce silhouette. The worg should be a single flat charcoal-gray silhouette, with only its eyes glowing smoky ice-blue. Keep the eye glow subtle and contained around the eyes, not beams or flames. Remove everything else from the reference: no rocks, moon, stars, terrain, lettering, or extra background shapes. No text yet. Practical, stark, and recognizable at patch size.

## Avoid

Rocks, moon, stars, terrain, extra background shapes, text, banners, or any decoration beyond the shield and its single blue border; cropping or changing the worg’s pose; cartoon or sports-mascot style, eye beams or flames, multiple colors in the worg, gradients, shading, texture, chrome effects, extra symbols, tiny details, extra animals, extra limbs, blood, gore, weapons, ornate Norse knotwork, runes, tattoos, or tribal patterns.

## Composition variants

1. **Subtle glow:** Preserve the reference worg’s charging pose; keep blue eye glow faint and tight.
2. **More visible glow:** Same shield, border, and worg; make the smoky blue eye glow slightly brighter but localized.
3. **No glow:** Same design, with plain ice-blue eyes if the glow harms clarity.
