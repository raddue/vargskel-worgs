# Sparri character notes: Rurik & Sten

Companion notes for the local *Long Rim* and *Solstice Rain* chapter references. These are working campaign notes, not a claim that either supplement establishes Rurik and Sten. Their first names are provisional; Chuck is the other player, not a character name.

## Local supplement references

- `The-Long-Rim.md` contains Sparri examples including a technoshaman running a business, the Kapkat board game, mercenary activity, and people with extensive saga tattoos. These show varied everyday and professional lives alongside familiar cultural motifs.
- `Solstice-Rain.md` includes the Rio Grande crew’s fondness for Kapkat, a Sparri-associated game.

## Character-use notes

- The brothers can share a family/clan tattoo element while their individual tattoos record different experiences. Tattoo coverage and symbolism can be personal; avoid treating every Sparri as identically tattooed.
- Sparri background does not prescribe a mercenary career, combat personality, faith, or mech-pilot role. Use only traits the players want.
- Possible hooks to discuss: a family saga tied to Yuga or Ynn; different views of domstol and clan authority; contrasting approaches to preserving or revising family traditions; one shared formative event remembered differently by each brother.
- Nothing about origin, age, appearance, religious belief, profession, or past conflict is settled yet. Develop these with both players before writing definitive portrait descriptions.
