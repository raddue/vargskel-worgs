# Rurik Vargskel

Rurik and his brother Sten belong to the Vargskel family, part of the larger Clan Eirikssaga. The clan takes its name from Eirik, a distant founder. Their family feud with another, still-unnamed family dates back to the Familiekrig Saga. Rurik’s great-grandfather defended the Vargskels during that era and passed the grievance down. No one remembers what started it. The families keep things civil in public, then quietly undercut each other’s contracts and make shipments or useful information disappear. Now and then, a dispute turns violent. Some confrontations have left serious injuries, though the feud usually stays below open warfare. Rurik became an active fixer in this rivalry, arranging diversions and disrupting the other family’s deals. He sees it as family duty and enjoys the game of outmaneuvering his rivals. Sten is more direct when conflict comes to a head. The rival family hit the Vargskels hardest by poaching a valuable shipping broker and a respected community patron. The broker took discreet routes and carriers with them, exposed how the family worked, and left a hole in its income. The patron’s defection humiliated the Vargskels in public and cut off practical support and introductions. The family hid the damage while quietly rebuilding, then retaliated by making the rivals lose a major contract. Rurik planned the slick operation, using his contacts and system access to uncover real missed deliveries, then planting forged details alongside the real records to make the rival family look even less reliable. Sten knew the evidence included forged details and brought it to the contract broker face-to-face, helping make the case stick. The broker pulled the major contract from the rivals. The job went almost exactly to plan. The rival family suspects the Vargskels planted the forged details, but has never been able to prove it. When Rurik favors a clever play and Sten wants to confront someone directly, they trade barbs and usually compromise.

Their father, Håkon, prefers careful plans, favors, and patience. Their mother, Runa, is a warrior from an allied branch of the Vargskel family. She knows the feud well and has earned respect for her skill and for leading people through danger. Their paternal grandparent, Liv, told the boys the family’s version of their great-grandfather’s story. The rest of the family tree can wait. Rurik and Sten call their small security and escort outfit the **Vargskel Worgs**, a working name for now. Their logo is a crisp, modern military unit patch with an obvious shield shape. It features an illustrated worg, “VARGSKEL” in a tab across the top, and “WORGS” in a tab across the bottom, with a few small geometric symbols. The palette is charcoal, steel gray, and ice blue. The look is straightforward, not a snarling sports mascot, and has no tattoo motifs.

Rurik grew up into the diaspora and made his living as a gray-market fixer, brokering deals and connecting people who could not afford to be seen together. He learned to read people, organize a crew, and hack into systems when a job called for it. After the forged-evidence caper, the rival family suspected the Vargskels but could not prove it. Skirmishes and reprisals escalated until someone was shot. Rurik and Sten realized they had to leave and let the feud cool before it consumed their family. They took some of their ill-gotten gains and found work out in the Long Rim. Before founding their own outfit, the brothers joined a mercenary company on the ground crew. They took mixed contracts, then moved into technical work: Rurik handled computers, communications, and electronic warfare, while Sten specialized in mechanical work and field repairs. Their original company, Ironmark Services, eventually brought them into pilot training. One co-founder, Mara Venn, became Rurik’s mentor and helped him move from comms and electronic warfare into a mech cockpit. The other co-founder and their close friend Kade Orlov, a fellow pilot, also died in the ambush, which followed bad or incomplete intelligence. Rurik and Sten suspect someone deliberately set them up, but they have no proof. Two crewmates survived and remained independent allies: Tala Neri, Ironmark’s medic, and Vesper Korr, its quartermaster. The company was left with little equipment and money, so the brothers started over with what remained. Taking work in the Long Rim, they saved enough to buy their own mechs and founded a small, scrappy security and escort company. They built it from what little Ironmark left behind, plus contacts and trust earned on the Rim. Both brothers pilot for the outfit, which takes practical jobs and grows one contract at a time. How Rurik earned his reputation as a Lancer remains open.
