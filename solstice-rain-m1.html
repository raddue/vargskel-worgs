THE BRIEF

READ IT, THEN DROP THEM
TUNED FOR 5 PCS · SOFTENED
SHIPUNS Rio Grande, in orbit. Strike wings are exoatmospheric only; the ship itself is lightly armed. There is no orbital or air support once the PCs touch down.
VIPSCaptain Farris and Ambassador Bannerjee, both missing in the central district after the talks were hit. Secure then extract them.
FOBForward Operating Base Saber — the Amanta Motors factory pressed into service in Nov Elysia's industrial district. Rendezvous there.
ENEMYVestan Sovereignty (VSAF) — "roundheads", the aggressors. The LSA (Leandric States Alliance) soldiers are friendlies, not hostiles.
COMMSCity-wide jamming blanks all local comms and patches sensors. The assigned omnihook still reaches the Rio Grande over omninet, if someone tunes it.
ROEDo not engage unless fired upon. Priority is getting people home.
  • "Shit! Starboard engine's blown, stabilizers aren't responding! Mayday, mayday, this is Kingfisher 2-2!"

Give them a beat to get their bearings, take the dead pilots' dog tags, and form a plan. Then drop the first fight on them.

BEAT 1 · ON THE MOVE IN A MILITARY MANNER

THE BUS · NARRATIVE
FRAMINGBetween combats = a rest (Lancer p. 82) plus a narrative challenge. Nov Elysia: ash haze, abandoned cars, looping civil-defense loops, glass crunching under the mechs.
HOOKA stalled bus full of civilians, ringed by LSA soldiers trying to patch the engine. They throw hands up fast — "Don't shoot! There are civilians on board!"
ASKEscort them to a nearby shelter. Run it as an extended skill challenge (three stages) or as COMBAT 2 below — the skill route is for groups low on time or beat up.

BEAT 2 · BROKEN SKY

THE MISSILE BATTERY
FRAMINGAnother rest, then out into the rain. They're now within reach of the original drop point — and they see why this mission has been so hard.
SCENEA train depot on the most direct route to FOB Saber. A missile-launcher mech perched on a train car tracks an inbound Union dropship, fires, and downs it. Smoke rises in the distance.
STAKESSovereignty mobile missile batteries lock down the FOB Saber approach. Until this one is dead, no reinforcement can touch down near the base without being shot down.

GRUNT

Template · p.327. HP 1, cannot regain HP; successful saves prevent all damage. Never above 1 Structure/Stress; immediately destroyed by heat from outside its own systems. Cannot combine with Veteran, Squad, Elite, Ultra. Otherwise uses its class attacks and stats.

VETERAN

Template · p.332. +1 Structure and +1 Stress (total 2/2); choose one mech skill for +1 accuracy on checks and saves; choose one Veteran trait per tier (one at T1). Lightning Reflexes: heavy/superheavy attack misses automatically on d6 5+. Cut from tuned C3.

ELITE

Template · p.326. +1 Structure and +1 Stress (total 2/2); two separate turns each round; choose one extra optional class system. Cannot combine with Grunt or Ultra. Cut from tuned C3.

COMMANDER

Template · p.326. +1 Structure and +1 Stress (total 2/2); Voice of Authority 1/round: allied failed attack/check/save rerolled if Commander not Jammed; choose one Commander trait, inactive while Jammed. Quick March: 1/round protocol lets visible ally Boost. Cut from tuned C3.
