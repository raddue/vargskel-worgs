## THE SPINE (3 lines)

1. The Leandric States Alliance calls in the favour from op `002`, terms unspecified (`t7`).
2. They want Tarn surveyed quietly, and they want it not to be them who did it. Tarn is
   unvisited, unclaimed, outside all three faction zones (`campaign.js` → `locations`).
3. The wreck-strippers Granite traded fire with at Cairn Drift (`t4`, op `005`) are already
   on station. That is the conflict, not the planet.

**Default read of the Tarn mystery** (swap freely if you already have one): the depot wafer
from op `001` was routed through Tarn before it reached Nov Elysia (`t2`). The survey camp
that went dark was tracking that routing and got noticed.

## SCENE 1 — "TERMS UNSPECIFIED"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Kessen Relay (Leandric territory) |
| **When** | 5016U · D0xx, dock-side, no deployment |
| **Framing** | Negotiation. No mechs, hardsuits at most — same shape as op `002` |
| **Goal** | Get the contract, and get the terms *in writing* |
| **Exit when** | The crew accepts, refuses, or pushes for the real reason |

### Beats

**1. The summons.** A Leandric factor, **ONDRA VESH**, books a booth at Kessen and does not
offer a drink. She opens with the favour, plainly.

> "You owe the Alliance a favour. Terms were never specified. I am here to specify them."

**2. The ask.** Survey Tarn. Recover the camp's logs. Do not file the contract with Union.
The reason is deniable: Leandric cannot be seen surveying a planet they have no claim to.

- Read them: roll **Spot** / **Read a Situation** — Vesh is not lying, but she is holding one
  thing back. On a strong hit, the tell is that she already knows someone else is on Tarn.
- Push her: **Charm** or **Lead or Inspire** — she will admit the rival presence if pressed,
  but she will price the admission: the favour is now discharged *either way*.

**3. The catch.** Vesh offers the survey as full payment of the favour. It is not a fair
trade and she knows it, and she says so plainly. If the crew pushes back:

> "You owe one favour. This is one job. I am not obliged to make it a good job."

- Roll **Get a Hold of Something** or **Hack or Fix** if they try to read her datapad or the
  contract's metadata before signing — the transfer logs on it are the same format as the
  depot data from op `001`. That is the first thread pull toward `t2`.

**4. Sign or walk.** If they sign: Vesh hands over a survey beacon code and a heading, and
leaves. If they refuse: she discharges the favour anyway and notes that Union has a standing
salvage bounty on Cairn Drift — meaning the crew's next payday is the people who shot at them.

### Branches

- **They ask for support** → Vesh offers fuel and a berth at Kessen. Nothing else. She will
  not commit Leandric hulls to a job she denies running.
- **They ask who else is interested** → she deflects. On a strong roll she names Union's
  salvage registry, not the strippers. It is still true, just incomplete.
- **They go hostile** → she is not a combatant and does not fight. There are two Leandric
  security mechs on the dock (use tier-1 **Assault** ×2 in Comp/Con). A fight here burns
  op `002`'s goodwill and closes Leandric supply for the arc.

### Rules calls in this scene

- Trigger rolls are narrative: **1d20 + trigger**. Accuracy/difficulty as usual.
- Only two of the six pilots have triggers filed in `campaign.js` (frisky, dusty). **See the
  gap note at the end before you run checks against patch, latch, wren or bulwark.**

## SCENE 2 — "TARN APPROACH"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Tarn orbit → the survey camp on the surface |
| **When** | Same day, transit handled in a line or two |
| **Framing** | First contact. Mechs are hot but not committed |
| **Goal** | Get the camp logs before the rival crew does |
| **Exit when** | The logs are recovered, destroyed, or traded away |

### Beats

**1. Arrival.** Tarn is quiet and wrong-shaped: a survey camp with its lights on and its
beacon off. The Leandric beacon code is stale by eleven days.

- **Get a Hold of Something / Hack or Fix** (frisky, or anyone with a console): the camp's
  logs are half-wiped, but the wipe is recent and interrupted. Whoever did it was still here
  when the wipe started.
- **Act Unseen or Unheard** (latch if triggers get filed): the rival crew's landing scars are
  on the wrong side of the ridge — deliberately out of line of sight of the camp's approach.

**2. The other crew.** **ASHGRAVE RECOVERY** — out of Cairn Drift, and the same hulls that
were stripping the wreck under Granite's guns in op `005`. They are not surprised to see
Granite. Their foreman, **ORRIN BLUNT**, treats this as a business dispute and opens with a
split offer before anyone draws.

> "Half the camp each. You take the logs, we take the hulls. Nobody has to be stupid today."

- **Charm / Spot** — Blunt is honest about the offer and about having a Union salvage claim.
  He is lying about only one thing: he was paid to get here *first*, not to get the logs.
- **Lead or Inspire** — Blunt can be moved to a genuine split if Granite offers the claim
  rather than the salvage. That closes op `006` without a shot and hands you `t4`'s answer.
- **Take Someone Out / Get a Hold of Something** — the payment he is hiding is a Union
  delegee's, which is the thread that pulls `t1` and `t2` together.

**3. The fight, if it comes.** Blunt's crew is a screening force, not a garrison.

| Sitrep | |
|---|---|
| Objective | Camp console (the logs). Both sides want it intact |
| Terrain | Ridge line between the approaches; wrecked survey hulls = hard cover, difficult terrain |
| Opfor | 3× tier-1 **Assault**, 1× tier-1 **Support** on the ridge. Statblocks from Comp/Con |
| Reinforcements | 1× tier-1 **Ronin** on round 3 if Ashgrave's console attempt fails — their closer |
| What flips it | Killing the Support drops the ridge's accuracy edge. Blunt withdraws at 1 Structure |
| Wrecks | Wrecks are difficult terrain and hard cover, do not block LoS, and apply to pilots inside |

**4. The logs.** Whatever the outcome, the recovered logs contain the routing record from
`t2`: Tarn relayed the depot wafer's transfer. They also contain a partial roster of who
requested it — one name is a Union delegee, which is why Blunt was paid to beat Granite here.

### Rules calls — the hacking one, resolved

The table claim in the AP session was that hacking something that is not an Elite leaves it
**Exposed**. That is a real interaction, not a house rule, and it works like this:

- **Invade** is a Tech Attack against a target in Sensors + line of sight. On a success the
  target takes **+2 Heat**, and you then pick an Invade option. **Fragment Signal** is always
  available: **Impaired + Slowed** until the end of the target's next turn.
- **Standard NPCs have 1 Stress.** A mech with 1 Stress that exceeds its Heatcap does **not**
  roll on the Stress table — **it just becomes EXPOSED** until it clears it with Stabilise.
- **Elites and Ultras have more Stress**, so they roll the table instead and can shrug it off.
- **EXPOSED (mechs only):** all non-Burn damage taken is **doubled before any reductions** —
  which is why it applies before Armour. Cleared by Stabilise.

So the play is: stack Invades on a standard frame until its Heatcap trips, then shoot it while
it is doubled. Two caveats worth saying out loud at the table:

- **NPC Invades are simplified**: their Fragment Signal is **+2 Heat and Impaired only — no
  Slowed**, and beyond that their Invade options only do what they say. NPCs use **Systems**
  in place of a Tech Attack stat.
- **Grunts are destroyed by any Heat** from anything other than their own weapons and systems.
  Do not let Ashgrave's grunts eat a hack and then keep acting.

Other calls likely to come up in scene 2:

- **Structure:** at 0 HP reset to full and roll **1d6 per Structure missing, take the lowest**.
  5–6 Glancing Blow (Impaired to end of next turn) · 2–4 System Trauma (lose a weapon or a
  system) · 1 Direct Hit (3+ Structure: Stunned · 2: HULL check or destroyed · 1: destroyed).
  **0 Structure = destroyed.**
- **Stress:** 5–6 Emergency Shunt (Impaired) · 2–4 Destabilised Power Plant (**Exposed**) ·
  1 Meltdown.
- **NPCs deal fixed damage and cannot normally crit**, and cannot Brace, Bolster, Overcharge
  or Self-Destruct unless a template says so.

*(Rules text checked against the LANCER rules compendium and the wiki status list; the AP's
"hacking a non-elite Exposes it" is the 1-Stress interaction above.)*
