FAMILY RECORD / VW-01

THE VARGSKEL BROTHERS

Rurik and Sten, from the old family feud to Ironmark Services and the company they built after it fell.

FAMILY AND FEUD

CLAN EIRIKSSAGA

The Vargskels belong to Clan Eirikssaga, named for the distant founder Eirik. Their feud with another, still-unnamed family dates back to the Familiekrig Saga. Their great-grandfather defended the Vargskels, and the grievance passed down, though no one remembers what started it. In public the families remain civil. Behind the scenes they interfere with contracts, shipments, and information; some confrontations have left serious injuries.

Their father Håkon prefers patience, careful plans, and favors. Their mother Runa is a warrior from an allied Vargskel branch, respected for her skill and for leading people through danger. Their paternal grandparent Liv told the brothers the family’s version of their great-grandfather’s story.

Rurik became a fixer in the feud, arranging diversions and disrupting the other family’s deals. He treats it as duty and enjoys outmaneuvering them. Sten is more direct. They trade barbs over approach and usually compromise.

THE BROKER JOB

REPRISAL

The rivals poached a valuable shipping broker and a respected community patron. The broker took discreet routes and carriers, exposed the Vargskels’ methods, and damaged their income. The patron’s departure cost them public standing, introductions, and support. The family concealed the losses while rebuilding.

Rurik then uncovered real missed deliveries by the rivals and planted forged details among the records. Sten knew about the forgeries and presented the evidence to a contract broker face-to-face. The rivals lost a major contract. They suspect the Vargskels altered the record, but cannot prove it.

Skirmishes and reprisals escalated until someone was shot. Rurik and Sten left with part of their gains to let the feud cool before it consumed their family. They found work in the Long Rim.

IRONMARK SERVICES

THE LOST COMPANY

The brothers joined Ironmark Services on its ground crew and took mixed contracts. Rurik moved into computers, communications, and electronic warfare; Sten specialized in mechanical work and field repairs. Ironmark trained them as mech pilots. Co-founder Mara Venn mentored Rurik as he moved from comms into the cockpit.

An ambush following bad or incomplete intelligence killed Mara, Ironmark’s other co-founder, and the brothers’ close friend Kade Orlov, a fellow pilot. Rurik and Sten suspect a deliberate setup but have no proof. Medic Tala Neri and quartermaster Vesper Korr survived and remained independent allies.

THE WORGS

STARTING OVER

Ironmark had little equipment or money left. The brothers took practical work in the Long Rim, saved for their own mechs, and founded the Vargskel Worgs, a small security and escort company built on what remained of Ironmark and the contacts and trust they earned on the Rim. Both brothers pilot for the outfit. Rurik’s path to his reputation as a Lancer has yet to be told.

The unit’s working name is Vargskel Worgs. Its patch uses a clear shield, an illustrated worg, VARGSKEL and WORGS tabs, small geometric marks, and charcoal, steel gray, and ice blue. It is a military unit patch, not a snarling mascot or a tattoo design.
