ESCORT · p.269
SITREPEscort (Lancer p. 269)
OBJECTIVE1× Bus (SIZE 2, HP 20). Get it to the Extraction Zone by the end of round 8
DEPLOYMENTPCs in the PDZ on the left, with the bus. Enemies in their EDZ between you and the EZ — rubble, overturned vehicles, and an improvised fortified firing position in their zone.
THE BUSMoves only when a character who started adjacent makes a standard move. Daisy-chain it forward. If it ends adjacent to characters from both sides, it stops until only one side remains. SIZE 2 can move through (not stop in) obstructions smaller than itself.
VICTORYBus reaches the EZ. Soldiers give the PCs the lay of the land — opens a bonus PDZ B in Combat 3. A shy passenger hands over a stuffed rabbit: one-use CUSTOM PAINT JOB.
DEFEATBus can't extract. Driver panics down a side alley; a collapse seals it behind them. Its fate is left unknown; PCs fall back toward their own objective. Destroying the bus = no victor.
