SITREP N/A · KILL THE RAINMAKER
OBJECTIVEEliminate the RAINMAKER (and the rest of the quarter). Killing the Rainmaker alone isn't instant victory — but it neutralizes the anti-air even if the PCs then retreat.
DEPLOYMENTPDZ A always available. PDZ B (direct sightline on the Rainmaker) only if one PC helped the bus in Beat 1. NPCs placed 10+ spaces from any PDZ, however you like.
TERRAINTrain cars 3 spaces high, big enough to block line of sight; a section of car has EVASION 5, HP 10 if they try to blast sightlines through. Rainmaker sits behind them.
GADGETSTurntable Control Panel (adjacent, MANIPULATORS or dismounted pilot: quick action, no check; otherwise full action AGI check) rotates a car 90°. Crane Control Zone: once per combat, interface as a quick action to lift a designated car off the map entirely.
VICTORYBattery dead, corridor open — reinforcements land safely; FOB Saber is clear. Kill every enemy by end of round 5 → one extra EXOTIC GEAR selection at downtime.
DEFEATRainmaker survives (or they retreat). Reinforcements must divert or risk being shot down; the PCs reach FOB Saber late, weary, wounded, by a longer route.
